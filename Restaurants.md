# Restaurant Research — South Kolkata (Italian & Chinese, Vegetarian focus)

Compiled: 2026-10-16

## About this research

Goal: find restaurant experiences in **South Kolkata** for **Italian** and **Chinese**
food, with a focus on **vegetarian** options.

### Sources and what worked

| Site | Result | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| tripadvisor.com | Blocked | Served a DataDome CAPTCHA ("Access is temporarily restricted"). CAPTCHAs cannot be solved, so no data was retrieved. |
| zomato.com | Unreachable | DNS resolves (Akamai edge) and TCP connects in ~0.15s, but 0 bytes are ever returned — Akamai Bot Manager silently tarpits/drops this network's IP. |
| Google Maps | Worked | All data below comes from Google Maps live results (ratings, cuisine tag, street address, hours, short descriptions). |

### How to read the data

- Ratings are Google Maps star ratings at time of capture.
- "South Kolkata" classification is inferred from the street address.
- Veg notes: restaurants tagged *Vegetarian / Vegan / Pure Veg* on Google are
  veg-only. For mainstream Italian/Chinese places, veg availability is inferred
  from cuisine type, not a verified menu — confirm before a dedicated veg visit.

## Recommendations

**Italian (with good veg menus):**
- Mirabelle (4.7, Keyatala Rd) and Viaggio Gustoso (4.8, Sadananda Rd) — highest
  ratings and substantial vegetarian menus.

**Chinese (vegetarian):**
- Dedicated veg spots: Kailash Parbat – Pure Veg (4.8) and The Flaming Bowl (4.3).
- Best regular Chinese with good veg options: On Daa Wok (4.7, Gariahat).

**If you want pan-Asian veg beyond Chinese:** Mizuki (4.8, vegan).
